## Empirical test (#10): 12 real failed txs, 2026-10-07

Script `scripts/facts-failed.ts`, output `results/facts/failed-tx-fee-check.json`. Scanned 35 recent blocks (head 111,218,869) with `eth_getBlockReceipts`. Selected failed txs (`status 0x0`) where the sender sent exactly one tx in the block, is not the recipient of any tx in that block, and sent value 0, so the balance change between the end of block N-1 and N (`eth_getBalance` at N-1 and N) is attributable to the fee. In all 12 cases: `receipt.gasUsed == tx.gas`, and `balance(N-1) - balance(N) == tx.gas x receipt.effectiveGasPrice` to the wei. All type 2 txs.

## Fresh network measurements (2026-10-07)

- **Failure rate now:** 182 failed of 6,729 txs (**2.7%**) in 300 blocks spaced 66 apart over the last 20k blocks (`failrate-sample.json`). The Jan figure of 6.0% is stale as a "current" number. A second 35-block scan gave 16/651 (2.5%).
- **Burn per failed tx now:** mean **0.198 MON** (36.08 MON over 182 failures), and effective prices of 108-180 gwei, so the PRD's "about 0.03 MON for a 300k-gas call" is right only for a small, minimum-fee tx; real failures are often 1-2M gas limits. Failed txs carry 3.2% of all declared gas.
- **Block time:** 301.9 ms average over 20,000 blocks.
- Sample sizes are small for rates; use the D0-D2 index for the headline numbers.

## Things in the PRD that must change or be softened

1. **Airdrop farmer ($112k):** unverified, wrong explorer cited by the source, address truncated. Remove from the demo; keep at most as a hedged aside.
2. **"The docs say a reverted tx pays the full limit":** the docs say the limit is charged; the revert consequence is our (verified) inference. Cite the rule, then our 12-tx check.
3. **"About 5.4% were logic reverts (races, slippage, liquidations...)":** Category says only "other execution errors". Mark it as our reading.
4. **6.0% failed:** correct for 12-18 Jan, but today it is about 2.7%. Say "Jan 2026" and lead with our live number.
5. **44 TPS / 7%:** SQD's Aug figure is accurate as quoted but stale: Oct 2026 is about 78 TPS and 12% (limit-based). Block gasUsed on Monad is the sum of tx gas limits.
6. **Per-tx loss "about 0.03 MON":** observed mean failed-tx burn is about 0.2 MON (range 0.0076-0.29 in our sample). Fix the "who pays" and Q&A wording.
7. **Blockaid on Monad:** no evidence found. **Blocknative:** reported discontinued (secondary source only). **Flashbots Protect:** not re-fetched here.
8. **Agent Wallet interception:** only `wallet-submit` being "policy-gated by Agent Wallet" is documented; intercepting other sends is not (K3 stays open).
9. **newPendingTransactions:** it is silently accepted, not rejected. Anyone testing will see a subscription id and no events.
10. **Receipts mask gas used:** TRUE and measured (100% of 7,000+ receipts checked had gasUsed == limit).
